# Comparison Framework: Traditional Platform vs. AI Agent

A shared rubric for comparing incumbents with agent-based alternatives in every case study. Score each 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) for the consumer, with a source or reasoning behind each score.

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | What it means | How to evidence it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cost | Total price paid, including fees and markups | Price comparisons, fee structures |
| 2 | Time / effort | Steps and time needed to complete the task | Walk through both journeys yourself |
| 3 | Choice / coverage | How much of the market the consumer can see | Inventory coverage, partner lists |
| 4 | Personalization | How well the result fits this user's preferences | Product features, demos |
| 5 | Reliability | Does it complete the task correctly? | Reviews, error reports, our own tests |
| 6 | Trust / accountability | Recourse when something goes wrong | Refund policies, support, liability terms |
| 7 | Privacy | What data is collected and shared | Privacy policies, data access needed |
| 8 | Neutrality | Is it steering users toward paying partners? | Business model, disclosures |
