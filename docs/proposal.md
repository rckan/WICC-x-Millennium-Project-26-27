# Proposal: The Impact of Agentic AI on Service-Based Industries

## Research question

To what extent is the rise of agentic AI disrupting traditional service-based industries, and how is this transformation affecting consumer behavior, business performance, and employment?

## Overview

Our project will explore how the rise of agentic AI is transforming service-based industries, particularly businesses that rely on acting as intermediaries between consumers and services. As AI agents become increasingly capable of completing tasks independently, such as booking travel, comparing prices, managing subscriptions, and negotiating rates, traditional platforms like Expedia and Booking.com may face significant disruption.

## Key areas of exploration

1. **Rise of Agentic AI:** How AI is evolving from providing recommendations to independently executing tasks on behalf of consumers. → [`research/themes/01-rise-of-agentic-ai.md`](../research/themes/01-rise-of-agentic-ai.md)
2. **Industry Disruption:** How AI agents could reshape industries such as travel booking, subscription management, insurance, and financial services. → [`research/themes/02-industry-disruption.md`](../research/themes/02-industry-disruption.md)
3. **Economic Impact:** How increased automation and reduced reliance on intermediaries could affect company revenues, business models, and employment. → [`research/themes/03-economic-impact.md`](../research/themes/03-economic-impact.md)
4. **Consumer Benefits and Risks:** Potential cost savings and convenience, alongside concerns about privacy, reliability, and trust. → [`research/themes/04-consumer-benefits-and-risks.md`](../research/themes/04-consumer-benefits-and-risks.md)
5. **Future of Service-Based Businesses:** Whether existing companies will adapt by integrating AI agents or become increasingly obsolete. → [`research/themes/05-future-of-service-businesses.md`](../research/themes/05-future-of-service-businesses.md)

## Research approach

We plan to examine real-world examples of emerging AI agents, analyze industry research and economic forecasts, including reports from Goldman Sachs, and compare traditional service-based platforms with AI-powered alternatives.

## Project goal

Our goal is to understand how agentic AI is reshaping consumer behavior and traditional business models, while evaluating its broader economic implications and the future of service-based industries.
